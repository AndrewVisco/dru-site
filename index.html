Our story

I grew up dealing with food allergies, always noticing products other people could enjoy that I couldn’t.

When I got into fitness and nutrition, the protein category was the most frustrating. Many high-protein bars contain dairy or nuts, and many allergy-friendly options didn’t have the protein I was looking for.

So I started Dru to make the bar I always wanted: high-protein, plant-based, and designed with more people in mind.

People shouldn’t feel excluded from everyday foods because of dietary restrictions.

That’s why Dru is made for everyone.

Andrew ViscoFounder, Dru
